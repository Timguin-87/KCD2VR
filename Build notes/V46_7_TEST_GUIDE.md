# V46.7 test guide

Build fresh, launch KCD2, verify F7 menu still works, then press F8 once.

During phases 1-5 note which mono view has the best scale, edge geometry and no black bar.
During phases 6-9 note any visible change at all; these are observer phases and may remain visually mono.
During phase 10 stay idle and hold the controllers naturally for 10 seconds.

Useful shorthand:
`mono best = #`; `black bar = yes/no`; `warp = better/same/worse`; `native 6/7/8/9 = change/no change`; `VRIK idle completed = yes`.

After phase 10 the mod restores the pre-test mono FOV/framing settings.
